## The readings, fixed before any arm is launched
The unit is one arm's transcript, cut at the compaction boundary. *After* = from the boundary to the arm's first write in turn 2 (a `Write` or `Edit` call, or a shell command that writes — the 21.2 census's definition), or to the end of the turn if it never writes.

| | metric | read from | what it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(i)** | **whole by its own act**: *after*, the arm reads `.bemyagent/docs/00-ai-rules.md` whole — one access with no offset, limit or slicing command, no truncation sign in its result, all eight section headings in the result. Classes: `whole` · `slice` (it read part and never the whole) · `none` (no access) | transcript | the outcome |
| **(ii)** | **handed back**: all eight section headings appear *after* in something other than the result of the arm's own access (an attachment, the summary) | transcript | eligibility — such an arm is not an occasion and leaves (i) |
| **(iii)** | the compaction is proven: a boundary in the compaction call's stream output or in the transcript; `pre_tokens` where given | launcher output, transcript | eligibility — no proof, no turn 2, the arm leaves (i) |
| **(iv)** | turn 1's restore: `whole` / `slice` / `none` before the first write of turn 1 | transcript | reported; expected `whole` in 6 of 6 |
| **(v)** | the arm's tool memory after turn 1: exists, number of files | launcher | reported beside (i) |
| **(vi)** | cost: weighted tokens per call, turn 2 apart | launcher output | priced in the verdict |
| **(vii)** | turn 2's ceremony: a new leaf under `.bemyagent/work/` between the turn-1 snapshot and the final tree; `reset` new in `client/` or `server/` | snapshot, final tree | reported, no prediction — what a restore that is not whole costs downstream |

**The verdict**, on (i) over the eligible arms, control read first, by `harness/README.md`'s table:

| C `whole` | L `whole` | Verdict | What follows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0/3 | 3/3 | the mechanism | the line is merged into both files on `main`; `BEMYAGENT.md` changes, so a tag is due — commit and tag are the user's |
| 0/3 | 0/3 | null | the branch is dropped; `decisions/rejected.md` gets the line with its reopening condition |
| 3/3 | any | ceiling | the branch is dropped — after a compaction produced this way clean arms already read the rules whole; the census's failure is not reproduced by this proxy, and the record says *ceiling*, not *null* |

## Amendment 1 — 2026-11-15, before any launch
Written by the designer after the independent VERIFY of the apparatus (`03e_apparatus_verify.md`: cleared for the pilot with caveats, **not** cleared for the six). Nothing has been launched and no arm's result is known. The readings above stand except where a point below replaces them; the letters in brackets are the verifier's findings.

- **A1 — (i) and (iv) are classed by what the access delivered, not by its form** (D1, N1). `whole` = one access by the arm, resolved to its rules file, whose result holds **every non-empty line of that file, in order**. `slice` = an access whose result holds at least one of its lines and not all. The form (no parameters / offset or limit / shell command) is recorded beside the class and decides nothing. Why: the line under test says *first line to last*; a read with a limit larger than the file obeys it, and the eight-headings test passed a result with lines cut or elided. Several slices that together cover the file stay `slice`, noted `union`: the line asks for one read.
- **A2 — a fourth class, `attempted`** (M3): an access to the rules file whose result holds none of its lines (the tool's "unchanged since last read" answer, an error). The arm leaves (i) — the tool withheld the text, not the arm — and is reported per condition beside the counts. Whether the tool answers this way after a compaction is unknown and the pilot may not show it; a probe arm was considered and not built (what it would show does not carry over to a session that has written files since its read).
- **A3 — delivery the reader does not resolve** (M4): when no counted access is `whole` and the result of another call of the arm, inside the window, holds every line in order, the arm is flagged `delivered-unresolved` and classed by the hand-read under a rule fixed now: the call's target was the arm's rules file (a glob, a loop, a symlink, a script that opens it) → `whole`; its target was another file that embeds the text (`BEMYAGENT.md`) → `none`, reported.
- **A4 — the window** (M5, N3): it starts at the boundary the compaction call wrote — the launcher's count before the call, plus one — not at the last boundary of the transcript. It ends at the first write **that succeeded**: a `Write` or `Edit` that returned an error is not a write. An access delivered by the same call as the first write, or issued before it in the same assistant message, is inside the window. A further boundary inside the window is flagged.
- **A5 — eligibility** (M2, N8, N4): an arm is in (i) only if (a) the compaction is proven, as before; (b) turn 2 ran in the same session — its prompt is a user record after the boundary and `t2.json` carries turn 1's session id — and did not fail before any tool call; (c) its certificate is clean; (d) the rules were not handed back whole. (ii) counts **lines, not headings**: `handed back` = n of N non-empty lines of the rules file found outside the results of the calls the arm made **after the boundary**: in records after it, or in records the compaction kept across it — a result of a turn-1 call that the compaction kept counts, since it puts the text back without the arm acting. N of N → not an occasion. 0 < n < N is reported per arm and does not exclude it: both conditions get the same hand-back, and a restore that starts from part of the rules is still a restore.
- **A6 — the pilot's gates** (M1, M6): `PILOT OK` also needs (iv) = `whole`, no `attempted` access, and `handed back` = 0 of N with nothing of the rules kept across the boundary. Any other hand-back stops the round for the designer's ruling, written here as a dated amendment before the six. So does a turn 2 with no tool call (in the six such an arm stays, class `none`, flagged). A second model in a call's usage is reported and does not stop: the pin is read on the assistant records of the transcript. A stop prints which check failed.
- **A7 — the launcher** (N5): a repeated arm name is run once; each call has a time limit (60 minutes by default), and a call that hits it ends its arm like a failed call.
- **A8 — step 9's hand-read** (N2): before a counted `whole` is accepted, the shell commands that precede it in the window are read for a write the census's definition does not see (`touch`, a script that writes, `git apply` …); one found moves the class to `none` and is named.

Kept as built: the pilot is not counted; `offset: 0` is no offset; a stream-only proof leaves the arm out of (i); an arm whose session id changes at the compaction stops. `harness/README.md`'s table has `3/3 | 3/3` for the ceiling; the wider row above (`3/3 | any`) is the one this round is read by.

## Amendment 2 — 2026-11-15, before any launch
Written by the designer after the second independent pass (`03f_apparatus_reverify.md`: cleared for the pilot with caveats, **not** cleared for the six). Nothing has been launched and no arm's result is known. Amendment 1 stands except where a point below replaces it; the letters in brackets are the verifier's findings. Two of them are defects of Amendment 1's wording, not of the build: A2 was too wide (R1), A5(b) too narrow (R4).

- **B1 — `attempted` is narrowed** (R1; replaces A2's definition). `attempted` = an access to the arm's rules file **of a whole form** — a `Read` with no offset and no limit, or with ones that reach both ends of the file; a shell command that prints the file with no range, filter or count (`cat`, `nl`, a pager) — whose result holds none of its lines: the tool's "unchanged" answer, an error. Only this leaves (i). An access of any other form that delivers no line (a search with no hit, a count, a range past the end, `grep -q`, the `Grep` tool answering with a file name) is **not an access for (i)**, whatever its exit code: it is listed with its form, and the arm's class comes from its other accesses — `none` if there are none. Why: the verdict counts `whole` against everything else; an arm that searched the rules had the occasion and did not read them, and dropping it loses arms unevenly if the line changes how often an arm searches. A form the reader cannot place is not `attempted`: it is flagged for the hand-read, which rules by this paragraph.
- **B2 — what (ii) counts** (R2; replaces A5's count). The lines counted are the non-empty lines of the rules file of **20 characters or more** that the arm's own instruction file, as the turn-1 snapshot holds it, does not hold — the tool loads that file in every session, in both conditions: its lines are never restored, they are always there. M = their number; `handed back` = n of M. Hits on the shorter lines (two fences, a table rule) are printed and decide nothing.
- **B3 — where (ii) looks** (R3b; replaces A5's "outside the results of the calls the arm made after the boundary"). Every record after the boundary counts, of any type and under any key, and so does every record the compaction kept — except two things: the text the arm's own pinned model wrote in its assistant records, and the result of a call issued after the boundary (matched by the call's id; the same result under another key of the same record is the same result). A result with no matching call after the boundary counts. Why: if the rules come back in a shape the reader skips, six arms read nothing in both conditions and the round says "the line does nothing".
- **B4 — a threshold for a partial hand-back** (R6; completes A5). n of M is printed beside each arm's class in the verdict. An arm with **half of M or more** handed back is not an occasion and leaves (i), as M of M does. Below half it stays. The number is arbitrary and is fixed before any result; the pilot stops on any n > 0 (A6), so if the pilot's number is not 0 the designer rules again, dated, with it in view. A null verdict in which a counted arm has n > 0 is written "null at a hand-back of up to n of M", never "the line does nothing".
- **B5 — the pilot's gates, added** (R3a, R3c, R3d). `PILOT NOT OK` also when: a preserved field is present, not empty, and a record it names is not in the file (ids counted once); the context did not shrink at the boundary, or the two token numbers cannot be read; the boundary record's metadata holds a key the reader does not know — the key's name is printed: a field named otherwise is how a kept record would pass unseen. In the six, an arm whose context did not shrink leaves (i) under A5(a): that is not a compaction.
- **B6 — a turn 2 that failed** (R4, R11; replaces A5(b)'s "did not fail before any tool call"). An arm whose turn 2 failed — an error result, an exit code other than 0 — and made no write that succeeded leaves (i), reason named: its window never closed. One that failed after a write that succeeded stays: its window is complete. A turn 2 killed by the time limit leaves an empty `t2.json`, so A5(b)'s session check cannot be made: the arm leaves, named "turn 2 killed by the time limit", not "turn 2 did not run".
- **B7 — the compaction call** (R5, R12; completes A7). An exit code other than 0 from the compaction call ends the arm: no turn 2 is launched, and the reader leaves out an arm whose status carries one, whatever the proof says. A time limit that is not a positive integer is refused.
- **B8 — the window, five details** (R7a, R7c, R8, R9, R10). A shell command that writes is a write whatever its exit code: the file was written. Inside one assistant record the order of the blocks decides what was issued before the write. A call that names the rules file **and** a file that embeds the rules is not counted `whole` by the reader: it is flagged and classed by the hand-read under A3's rule. The rules' lines are taken from the turn-1 snapshot — what the file held when the window opened; a rules file the arm changed in turn 1 is flagged. A certificate that fails only on calls made after the window's end does not take the arm out of (i): what an arm did after its reading cannot have changed it; the arm is flagged and its (vii) is read with that in mind.
- **B9 — a second pilot** (R13). No pilot is cleared and run again under its name: the evidence of one that stopped stays. `setup.py` builds `q901`, `q902`, `q903`, all C, none counted; a pilot that stops for a reason of the apparatus is followed, after the fix, by the next name.
- **B10 — the added line, whole** (R16). `setup.py` refuses to build unless the sha1 of the line added on `bma/21.4`, without its newline, is `c39979f0fa5bc245fc6454d087ef5fb7d8754cc3`.
- **B11 — the under-powered outcome, named now** (the verifier's note on the probe arm). A C pilot that never re-reads cannot show what the tool answers to a re-read after a compaction; the six may be the first sight of it. If the tool withholds, the arms that re-read leave (i) as `attempted` — mostly L arms if the line works — and the round ends with fewer than three eligible arms in a condition: *under-powered* by the table, written "the tool withheld the re-read: not tested by this proxy". What follows is approach B or a probe, not a verdict on the line.
- **B12 — three choices the builder made where B3 and B8 were open, ruled** (2026-11-15, after its fourth pass, before any launch). (a) The count of (ii) that decides is taken from the boundary to the end of the window: a hand-back after the arm's first write cannot have removed the occasion. The same count over the rest of turn 2 is printed beside it, and the pilot stops on either. (b) Records that are stored copies of a request stay out of the count — a copy made after the arm's own calls holds their results — with one exception: a copy that lies after the boundary and before the arm's first call after it holds only what was handed back, so it counts. The number of copies left out is printed. (c) A shell write with no result record is not a write, as R7b.

Left as they are, known: a `Write` with no result record is not a write (R7b); a whole delivery in JSON-escaped form is `none` (R14); each stop of R15 needs a ruling if it fires, not a re-run; A8 stays a step of the hand-read.
